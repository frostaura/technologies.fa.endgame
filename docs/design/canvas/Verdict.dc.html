Verdict · do not build

Do not build this as its own app.

Ladders for racket clubs are already sold, and nothing in the idea is something they lack. The one real gap is booking the court in the same step, and you already own that inside Crusaders.

Who already does this

  • RankedinLadders, leagues and rankings for racket clubs.
  • SportyHQClub management with box leagues and ladders.
  • PlaytomicBookings and matches with player levels.
Sample findings for this mock-up.

What gap is left

A challenge that books the court in the same step. None of the three ties the ladder to a club’s own court schedule.

That is a feature, not a product. It only has value where the booking system already lives.

Who would want it

Members of a club that already uses Crusaders. No one outside that club has asked for it.

No evidence of demand was found. That counts against building.

What would change the verdict

Build it as a feature of Crusaders Squash Control. Same members, same courts, no new sign-in. Estimated 180 credits instead of 520 for a separate app.

Change it: add to Crusaders

Overruling is your call. The project will carry a note that research advised against it.
